When you receive a medical order, the next step is not always obvious. You may need to price several exams, send a photo, and help a family member at the same time.

Before sharing it, take a few seconds to pause. This is not about interpreting the medical indication. It is about making sure the image is clear and reaches the right channel.

## Five checks before sending it

### 1. Use the official channel

Do not post a medical order in comments or send it to a number you cannot verify. Comments are public spaces, and an order may contain personal information.

### 2. Look for good lighting

Avoid strong shadows, reflections, or low light. A clearer image makes it easier for a person to review its contents.

### 3. Include the complete document

Before pressing send, check that you did not crop a corner, a line, or an important page. If the order has more than one page, make sure they are all included.

### 4. Review the visible details

Look at the image one more time and confirm that it belongs to the right person. Do not accidentally send someone else’s document.

### 5. Keep clinical questions separate

A tool can help organize a quotation process, but it does not replace medical guidance. If you do not understand an instruction, ask your healthcare professional.

## Clarity before speed

A good photo does not guarantee a result or replace human review. It only removes an avoidable source of friction: having to request the document again because it arrived incomplete, dark, or through the wrong channel.

The rule is simple: official channel, complete document, and reviewed details before sending.
